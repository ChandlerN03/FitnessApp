# FitTrack

A sleek and simple fitness tracking app built with **React Native**. FitTrack helps users stay on top of their health goals by tracking **calories**, **water intake**, and offering **personalized workout recommendations**.

## Features

- **Calorie Tracker**  
  Log your daily meals and keep track of your calorie intake.

- **Water Intake Monitor**  
  Stay hydrated! Set water goals and log your daily water consumption.

- **Workout Recommendations**  
  Get tailored workout suggestions based on your activity level and fitness goals.

- **User-Friendly Interface**  
  Built with React Native for a smooth, cross-platform experience on both iOS and Android.
